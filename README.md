## 📖 About the Game

**Hero's Quest** is an educational trivia adventure game designed for children ages 5+. Guide your hero across a magical road by answering trivia questions correctly, collect coins, earn streaks, and unlock a fun tic-tac-toe minigame!

Perfect for:
- 🎓 Learning while having fun
- 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family game nights
- 📱 Playing on any device (desktop, tablet, mobile)
- 🧠 Building knowledge in math and science

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Main Trivia Game
- **3 Difficulty Levels:**
  - 🌱 **Easy** (14 math + 12 science questions)
  - ⭐ **Normal** (13 math + 13 science questions)
  - 🔥 **Hard** (14 math + 14 science questions)

- **Interactive Gameplay:**
  - Guide the hero across a road by answering 5 questions
  - Answer a final "boss question" to open the treasure chest
  - 5 hearts (lives) to complete each game
  - Beautiful animations and visual feedback

### 🐕 Pet Companion
- A cute dog that follows your hero on the journey
- Hops to celebrate correct answers
- Jumps with joy when you win!

### 💰 Coin Rewards System
- Earn coins for every correct answer:
  - 🌱 Easy: 1 coin per question
  - ⭐ Normal: 2 coins per question
  - 🔥 Hard: 3 coins per question
- **Bonus:** Earn extra coins for 3+ correct answer streaks! 🔥
- Track total coins earned throughout your game

### 🎵 Sound Effects
- Authentic Web Audio API sounds (no external files!)
- Happy beep for correct answers ✅
- Sad beep for wrong answers ❌
- Cha-ching sound for coin rewards 💰
- Victory fanfare when you win 🎉

### 🎯 Game Statistics
- Track accuracy percentage
- Monitor correct/wrong answer ratio
- Watch your streak grow
- View final statistics on win/lose screens

### 🎲 Minigame: Noughts & Crosses (Tic-Tac-Toe)
- Unlock after winning trivia
- Play against Smart AI opponent
- AI uses strategic decisions:
  - Tries to win first
  - Blocks your winning moves
  - Prefers center and corner positions
- Track wins vs losses across matches
- Unlimited replay rounds

### ⌨️ Keyboard Support
- **Number Keys:** Press 1, 2, or 3 to answer questions
- **Letter Keys:** Press A, B, or C to answer
- **Arrow Keys:** Navigate between answer options
- **Enter Key:** Confirm your selected answer

## 🎮 How to Play

1. **Start the Game:**
   - Visit [https://dienomb.github.io/hero-victor/](https://dienomb.github.io/hero-victor/)
   - Click the difficulty button (Easy, Normal, or Hard)

2. **Answer Questions:**
   - Read the trivia question
   - Click an answer button (or use keyboard shortcuts)
   - Correct answer = hero moves forward, earn coins! 💰
   - Wrong answer = lose 1 heart ❤️

3. **Progress:**
   - Answer 5 questions to reach the treasure chest
   - Answer the final "boss question" to open the chest
   - Reach 0 hearts = Game Over

4. **Unlock Minigame:**
   - After winning trivia, choose to play Tic-Tac-Toe
   - Play against the AI for unlimited rounds
   - Or try another trivia difficulty

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

**Sounds not working?**
- Check browser audio settings
- Ensure AudioContext is enabled
- Try refreshing the page

**Game won't load?**
- Clear browser cache
- Try a different browser
- Check internet connection

**Mobile display issues?**
- Try landscape orientation
- Check device zoom level (should be 100%)
- Use latest browser version
